Privacy Policy

Data Collection

Our games may collect anonymous gameplay-related data to improve game balance, performance, and overall player experience.

  • No personal information is collected

  • No precise location data

Data is used strictly for analytics such as: crash reports, level completion rates, and performance metrics.

Third-Party Services

Some games may use standard analytics or ad services provided by platform holders (Google Play, itch.io). These services follow their own privacy policies.

Ads & Third-Party Services

In addition to analytics, the game may display advertisements served by third-party advertising providers. These providers may collect information such as IP address, device identifiers, advertising IDs, cookies, or other non-personal usage data to deliver and personalize ads.

We do not control the data collection practices of third-party ad providers. For details about what data they collect and how they use it, please consult their privacy policies ( Google Ads, Unity). You can opt out of certain types of personalized advertising by adjusting your device or browser settings or by visiting YourAdChoices.

If we use specific ad partners, we will list them on this page along with links to their privacy policies. If you have questions about ads or third-party services, contact gamesgygk@gmail.com.
