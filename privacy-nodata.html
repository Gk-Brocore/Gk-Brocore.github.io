No Data Collection

This game does not collect, store, or transmit any data.

  • No personal data collected by the game

  • No tracking by the game itself

All gameplay runs entirely offline and locally on your device. However, if advertisements are displayed, they may be delivered by third-party services which could collect device or usage information as described below.

Ads & Third-Party Services

While the game itself does not collect, store, or transmit personal data, it may display advertisements served by third-party advertising providers. These providers may collect information such as your IP address, device identifiers, advertising IDs, cookies, or other non-personal usage data to deliver and personalize ads.

We do not control the data collection practices of third-party ad providers. For information about what data they collect and how they use it, please consult their privacy policies (Google Ads, Unity). You can opt out of certain types of personalized advertising by adjusting your device or browser settings or by visiting YourAdChoices.

If we use specific ad partners, we will list them on this page along with links to their privacy policies. If you have questions about ads or third-party services, contact gamesgygk@gmail.com.
